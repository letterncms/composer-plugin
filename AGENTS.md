# composer-plugin

Puts plugins in a site's `plugins/` folder and keeps its root files current. Two small classes in `src/`, and every site runs them, so keep it that way. To try a change, make a site next to your clone and point it there: `README.md` has the commands.

- **No plan files.** No specs, research or progress notes in the repository. Decisions go in commit messages.
- A change people can see or use gets a line under `## Unreleased` in `CHANGELOG.md`.
